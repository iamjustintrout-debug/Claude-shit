        Agitation

        1. Develop at most two rolls per tank.
        2. Fill the tank with water from the bath first to preheat it. Coloured water coming out is normal (anti-halation layer).
        3. After filling with developer or bleach fix, tap the tank on the table twice to dislodge air bubbles.
        4. Agitate continuously for the first 30 s, then gently tilt once every 15 s. Put the tank back in the water bath after each agitation.
        5. Or rotate continuously in a rotary processor.
        6. All step times include 10 s for filling and emptying the tank. Start the timer as you begin pouring.
        7. Development time is critical. Wash, bleach fix and stabilizer times are minimums and may be extended by up to 50%.

          Safety & disposal

          • Avoid skin and eye contact. Don't swallow the chemicals.
          • Keep away from children and food. Label every bottle clearly, and don't use bottles normally used for food or drink.
          • Don't pour photochemicals down the drain. Take them to a municipal hazardous-waste collection point.
